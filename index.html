Snake Crush Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play Snake Crush.

Snake Crush is an Android mobile game published by OddOrbit Games ("we", "us", or "our"). Players can enjoy the game without creating an account or providing their name, email address, or date of birth.

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. Audience

Snake Crush is intended for a mixed-age audience, including children under 13. The game does not request a player's age or date of birth and does not require registration.

2. Information stored on your device

Snake Crush stores information locally to operate the game and remember preferences, including:

  • Best score and local gameplay or session records.
  • Turning and control preferences.
  • Music and sound settings.

OddOrbit Games does not operate a game account or server that receives these local gameplay records.

Android backup: Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may back up some app data to your Google account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally removes local data from the device. Separate backup copies may remain until managed through your device or Google account.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Snake Crush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. We configure all advertising requests for child-directed treatment, a maximum G content rating, and disabled advertising personalization.

We do not intentionally request interest-based advertising or remarketing. The app is configured not to use the Android advertising ID.

Google's advertising technology may automatically process information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and troubleshoot advertisements, including:

  • IP address, which may indicate approximate location.
  • Ad views, interactions, and related app activity.
  • Device information and identifiers other than the Android advertising ID.
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

Such information may be transmitted to and processed by Google. For details, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information .

4. Children's privacy

We apply child-directed advertising restrictions to every player rather than collecting their age. The game does not ask children for names, email addresses, birth dates, or account details.

Google's advertising technology may still process the technical information described in Section 3. Parents and guardians may contact us with questions or concerns about children's privacy.

5. Consent and privacy choices

The game does not ask players to consent to personalized advertising. Its advertising configuration treats all players as under the age of consent and requests child-directed, non-personalized advertisements.

Players can manage certain device-level privacy and backup settings through Android and their Google accounts. You may contact us about privacy questions or applicable privacy rights.

6. Retention and security

Local gameplay data remains on the device until changed, cleared, or removed, subject to device backups. Google's retention of information processed through AdMob is governed by Google's applicable policies.

We do not intentionally transmit local gameplay records to OddOrbit Games servers. Google's advertising SDK uses encryption in transit for transmitted data as described in its documentation.

7. Third-party services

Snake Crush integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent-related technology. Google's applicable privacy policies also govern information it processes.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published at this address with a revised effective date where appropriate.
