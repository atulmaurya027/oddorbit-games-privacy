Snake Crush Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play Snake Crush.

Snake Crush is an Android mobile game published by OddOrbit Games ("we", "us", or "our"). Players can enjoy the game without creating an account or providing their name, email address, or date of birth.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

1. Intended audience

Snake Crush is currently designated for players aged 13–17 in its Google Play listing. The game does not request a player's age or date of birth and does not require registration. We do not use an in-game age-verification system.

2. Information stored on your device

Snake Crush stores information locally to operate the game and remember preferences, including:

  • Best score and local gameplay or session records.
  • Turning and control preferences.
  • Music and sound settings.

OddOrbit Games does not operate a game account or server that receives these local gameplay records.

Android backup: Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may back up some app data to your Google account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally removes local data from the device. Separate backup copies may remain until managed through your device or Google account.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Snake Crush integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising technology providers may automatically collect or process information to deliver, measure, secure, and troubleshoot advertisements. Depending on the applicable settings, device, region, and ad request, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may indicate approximate location.
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and related app activity.
  • Device information and identifiers, which may include advertising-related identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

We intend to apply age-appropriate advertising protections for our stated audience. The availability of personalized or non-personalized advertising, and the information processed, depend on the app's implemented advertising configuration and applicable Google requirements. We do not sell local gameplay records to advertisers.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

4. Young players' privacy

Snake Crush does not require players to provide names, email addresses, dates of birth, or account details. The current Google Play target audience is ages 13–17. Ad technology may nevertheless process the technical information described in Section 3. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy questions or concerns.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Snake Crush includes Google's consent-related technology to support applicable advertising privacy requirements. Depending on the player's location and applicable requirements, privacy notices, consent choices, or other options may be presented. Availability and behavior depend on the version of the app and its configured consent flow.

Players may also manage certain privacy and backup settings through Android and their Google accounts. Contact us with questions about privacy choices or applicable rights.

6. Retention and security

Local gameplay data remains on the device until changed, cleared, or removed, subject to device backups. Google determines retention of information processed through its advertising services according to its applicable policies.

We do not intentionally transmit local gameplay records to OddOrbit Games servers. Information sent by third-party advertising services is handled under their security practices and policies.

7. Third-party services

Snake Crush integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's consent-related technology. These services may process data independently of OddOrbit Games as described above. Google's applicable policies govern information it processes.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published at this address with a revised effective date where appropriate.
